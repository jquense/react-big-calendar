# onScroll

- type: `(scrollTime: Date) => void`

Callback fired when the time-grid scroll pane of the Day, Week and Work Week
views are scrolled. Receives the time of day at the top of the visible scroll
pane, the counterpart to [`scrollToTime`](./?path=/docs/props-scrolltotime--docs),
useful for persisting and restoring the scroll position.

The callback is not throttled; throttle or debounce it in your handler if needed.
